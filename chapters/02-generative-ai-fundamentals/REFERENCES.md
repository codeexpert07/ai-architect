# Chapter 2 — Further Reading

The following references provide authoritative or foundational material behind the concepts introduced in this chapter.

## Foundational Papers

1. **Vaswani et al., _Attention Is All You Need_** — foundational paper introducing the Transformer architecture.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

2. **Brown et al., _Language Models are Few-Shot Learners_** — influential work on large language-model scaling and in-context learning.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165

3. **Bommasani et al., _On the Opportunities and Risks of Foundation Models_** — broad treatment of foundation-model capabilities, risks, and ecosystem implications.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.07258

## Deep Learning

4. **Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville, _Deep Learning_** — comprehensive reference for deep-learning fundamentals.
   - https://www.deeplearningbook.org/

## Foundation Models

5. **Stanford Center for Research on Foundation Models (CRFM)** — research and educational material on foundation models.
   - https://crfm.stanford.edu/

## AI Risk Management

6. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** — risk-management perspective for trustworthy AI systems.
   - https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

## Reading Strategy

Read the papers selectively. For an AI architect, the goal is not to become a model researcher before designing applications; it is to understand the architectural consequences of model behavior, inference, context, evaluation, security, and operational trade-offs.

References are intentionally maintained separately from the main chapter so the chapter remains readable while still providing a path to authoritative material.
