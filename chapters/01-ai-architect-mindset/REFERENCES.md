# Chapter 1 — Further Reading

The following references provide canonical material behind the security, risk-management, and evaluation themes introduced in this chapter.

## Security

1. **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025** — canonical reference for LLM-specific application security risks including prompt injection, excessive agency, insecure output handling, supply-chain risks, and unbounded consumption.
   - https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-llm-applications-2025/

2. **OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications** — additional guidance for systems where models can plan, use tools, and take autonomous actions.
   - https://genai.owasp.org/2025/12/09/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-the-benchmark-for-agentic-security-in-the-age-of-autonomous-ai/

## AI Risk Management and Evaluation

3. **NIST AI 600-1 — Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile** — practical guidance for identifying and managing risks across the generative-AI lifecycle, including development, deployment, use, and evaluation.
   - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

4. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** — broader framework for trustworthy AI risk management.
   - https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

## Reading Strategy

These references are intentionally kept separate from the main chapter prose so the chapter remains readable while still providing a path to authoritative material. Future chapters should use the same pattern when external standards or frameworks materially support architectural guidance.
